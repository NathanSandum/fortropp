User-facing wording states outcomes as matters of fact ("The partial period November 30 (UTC) will be waived."), never the internal rule behind them ("comes to under $1, so it's waived").

**Why:** 2026-11-15, Add Card drawer waiver line; user: "We're not establishing a public facing rule of waiving things under $1 necessarily. Don't express it as a logic... Simply matters of fact. Nothing more." Stating a rule publicly turns an internal policy into a promise.

**How to apply:** drop "because/so/under X" clauses from copy; say what happens. Related: [[feedback-user-facing-wording]], [[feedback-no-information-from-absence]].
